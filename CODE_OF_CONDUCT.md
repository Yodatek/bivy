# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in bivy to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age,
body, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, social or economic status, nationality,
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We act and talk in ways that keep this community open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and
healthy.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- being kind and patient with other people;
- respecting other opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- owning our mistakes, apologizing to those they affected, and learning from them;
- putting what is best for the whole community first.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing other people's private information, such as a physical or e-mail address,
  without their explicit permission;
- any other conduct that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every space of the project, such as issues, pull requests,
discussions and reviews, and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's private reporting form](https://github.com/Yodatek/bivy/security/advisories/new),
and start the title with "Conduct". Every report is reviewed promptly and fairly, and the
maintainers keep the reporter's identity private.

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide what to do about behavior they find inappropriate, from a private
warning to a temporary or permanent ban from the project. They may remove or edit
comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and they
explain their decision when it helps.
